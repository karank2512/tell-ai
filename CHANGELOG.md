## [Unreleased]

### Fixed

- `tell crm hubspot push` no longer creates a duplicate company when it runs within seconds of a previous push (issue #5): HubSpot's search index lags creates, so a search miss now falls through to paging `GET /crm/v3/objects/companies` (100 per page, case-insensitive `domain` match, at most `LIST_PAGE_CAP` = 10 pages) before anything is created; the JSON line reports `listed: {pages, capped}` when the fallback ran. - Scoring rule: `funding` magnitude is `log10(total_offering_amount)` capped at 8, or 6 when the amount is undisclosed or zero (spec section 8); `form_d_filed` events flagged `is_amendment` never produce a signal.
- Scoring rule: `leadership` fires on `news_leadership` and on `form_8k_filed` with Item 5.02; `launch` on `news_launch` and flagship `github_release`; `expansion` on `news_expansion`; `tech_adoption` on `tech_mentioned_first_time` except first-poll baselines; `news_funding` carries no weight (Form D is the receipt).
- Scoring rule: a contribution is `w * magnitude * 0.5 ** (age / half_life)`, zero before the signal starts and beyond four half-lives (dropped from the list); the scoring instant for a date is the end of that UTC day.
- Scoring rule: `hiring_velocity` weighs 1.0 for a profile's target families and `other_families_weight` (0.3) otherwise; `tech_adoption` weighs the profile value only when the keyword is in `tech_keywords` or that list is empty (0 under `default`).
- Scoring rule: `score_crossed_threshold` fires on an upward crossing of the profile's `alert_threshold` (6.0) with a 7-day cooldown and a re-arm below `threshold - 0.5`, so an oscillation under half a point alerts once; companies outside the profile's `size_band` keep their stored raw score but never alert (unknown size counts as in band).
- Scoring rule: heat is a 0-100 percentile rank of raw scores within a watchlist (ties count half), display-only.

### Changed

- Resolution: alias hygiene (ADR 0033, proposed). No alias is shorter than 3 letters or digits, all digits, or only filler tokens (`of`, `ai`, `the`, `fund`, ...). A domain label is not an alias when it is a common English word of 4 letters or fewer or a platform's domain. A one-word alias is scored only against one-word names, so Form D fund names no longer hit `of` / `apple` as token supersets. The YC seeder skips companies whose website is on a platform domain (`skipped_platform_domain`). New dry-run-by-default `tell resolve prune-aliases`, `tell resolve close-junk-reviews` and `tell discover prune-platform-companies`. Why: production's 2026-10-13 Form D run sent 69 filings to review, all wrong. Offline, R5 on 300 Form D-like names drops from 184 to 6 with no correct link lost.
