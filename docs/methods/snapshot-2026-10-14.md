# Snapshot 2026-10-14: 1,000 active YC companies

Revised 2026-10-14 (v3): guessed boards now need domain evidence (ADR 0031); 28 guessed boards moved to review. The company that had no recorded outcome was re-discovered and now has one. Posting-level numbers (sections 2-6) count only boards still supported, so the 1,022 open postings on the 28 paused boards are left out. Every number below is recomputed on that state.

Coverage wording corrected 2026-10-19: this page no longer gives 16.1% or 13.7% as an estimate of true coverage, and the figure to quote is 130 of 1,000 (13.0%). No measured number changed.

One reproducible measurement of what tell sees on a random sample of active Y Combinator companies: how many have a job board tell can read, where the others hire, what is on the boards, how old the open postings are, and which roles and tools they mention. It re-runs the 2026-10-13 snapshot on the same 1,000 companies with the fixed collectors and the revised discovery, on a fresh database. Discovery and the poll ran 2026-10-14 06:00Z to 07:17Z. The v3 re-verification and re-discovery ran 08:12Z to 08:13Z, and the measurement at 08:14Z. Every percentage has its numerator and denominator beside it. This is a single snapshot: it has no velocity, reposts or posting lifetimes, because those need at least two polls.

## Question

For a random sample of active YC companies, what share has a job board on a documented public ATS API, and where do the others point? For the boards tell polled once: how many open postings are there, how stale are they, what is the role mix, which tech keywords appear, and how often is the same requisition listed twice?

## Cohort

The same 1,000 companies as the 2026-10-13 snapshot, from the same sample file.

- **Population.** The public YC directory mirror `https://yc-oss.github.io/api/companies/all.json`, downloaded once at 2026-10-14T03:10:17Z, 6,297 records, SHA-256 `8c237f109a33b9f2e36616dbcdc8c4b72c2a1f3f437c23b671c97e327018f411`.
- **Filter.** `status == "Active"` and `website` a non-empty string after stripping whitespace: **4,337** companies.
- **Sample.** Population sorted ascending by YC `id` (then `slug`), then `random.Random(20261013).sample(population, 1000)` (CPython 3.13.2). **N = 1,000.** Only `id`, `slug`, `name`, `website`, `batch`, `all_locations` and `status` were kept.
- **Seeding.** `tell discover seed-yc --file yc_sample.json` (the 2026-10-13 file, unchanged): read 1,000, inserted 1,000, skipped for unusable website 0, duplicate domains 0.

## What changed since 2026-10-13

The 2026-10-13 snapshot (same sample, alembic head and rate budget) is withdrawn from this directory because its sections 2-6 were partial. Four code changes separate the runs:

- **Collector contract fix.** Ashby `isRemote` and Greenhouse `location.name` may be `null`; on 2026-10-13 that failed 35 of 101 Ashby boards and 1 of 36 Greenhouse boards outright. A job that still fails validation is now skipped and counted instead of failing its board. Result here: 0 boards failed to parse and 0 jobs were skipped.
- **Discovery recall (ADR 0030).** When the first page that answers names no ATS, discovery now also reads the homepage, up to 2 same-site careers links and up to 5 same-site scripts. It records 18 more ATS vendors and YC Work at a Startup as `unsupported` instead of `no_board`. It also records Notion, Google Docs/Forms and LinkedIn links on the `no_board` marker and skips slug guesses after them. The slug list is unchanged.
- **Concurrent discovery.** Six domains in flight share the one 4 rps budget, with a 22-minute budget per batch and a commit per domain.
- **Ownership evidence for guesses (ADR 0031, v3).** A slug guess stays active only if the board's own public data points at the company's registrable domain. A guess that matches on name alone is paused and sent to review. A shared unsupported destination now gets its own row per company.

## Caveats

- **Which coverage figure to quote.** On 2026-10-14, 130 of 1,000 randomly sampled active YC companies (13.0%) had a Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby or SmartRecruiters job board linked to their site. This is the supported-board coverage figure to quote, as a count of verified boards and not an estimate of true coverage. 161 of 1,000 (16.1%) was the 2026-10-13 discovery run, before the deeper scan (ADR 0030) and ownership evidence for slug guesses (ADR 0031), and counted 88 page-linked boards plus 73 name-only slug guesses; 158 of 1,000 (15.8%) was the 2026-10-14 run before re-verification. Neither is quoted, because both count slug guesses whose ownership had not been checked against the company's domain, and ADR 0031's hand review found 21 of the 28 guesses that re-verification then paused to be other companies' boards. 130 is the same database after `tell discover reverify-guesses`: 106 page-linked boards plus 24 guesses with domain evidence (158 minus 28 paused). The 2026-10-13 hand audit found no supported board missed among 40 random no-board companies (0 of 40, Wilson 95% upper bound 8.8%); it ran before ADR 0030 and ADR 0031 and is not a coverage estimate for this run.
- **Guesses and the review queue.** Re-verification under ADR 0031 kept 24 of 52 guessed boards and paused 28. ADR 0031's hand review of those 28 found 21 other companies' boards, 5 the company's own and 2 unclear. ADR 0031 also reports that the rule rejects all 11 wrong guesses known from the audit and the recount, and keeps none of them. A kept guess is still only as good as the board's own link to the company's domain.
- **Paused boards' postings are excluded.** The 28 paused boards were polled before they were paused; their 1,022 open postings and 188 tech events stay in the database. Sections 2-6 leave them out, and they made up 28.0% of the 3,647 postings the poll stored. Revision 2 counted them.
- **The company without an outcome now has one.** Two sampled companies link the same Gem destination. Before ADR 0031 the second company's discovery overwrote the first company's row and left itself without a row, so every batch picked it again and the batch summaries count it three times (1,002 outcomes for 1,000 companies). Re-running discovery for that company alone recorded it as `unsupported` (Gem) under its own key. The other company's row still carries the second company's careers URL as `discovered_from`, written under the old behaviour; no count depends on it.
- **One Ashby board answered 404.** Discovery found it linked from the company's page, and its hosted page `jobs.ashbyhq.com/<name>` answers 200 and lists the company's jobs. Ashby's public posting API (`api.ashbyhq.com/posting-api/job-board/<name>`) answers 404 for the same name: the hosted page exists, but its posting API is not served. tell reads only the API, so the board is counted as supported and not polled.
- **Slug guesses now depend on the scan.** A company whose page points only at YC Work at a Startup or an unsupported ATS is no longer guessed. Seven companies with a guessed board on 2026-10-13 moved to `unsupported` this way. Some of those guesses may have been other companies' boards; this run cannot tell which.
- **Single snapshot.** One discovery pass and one poll; nothing here is a trend. Pages change between days: the audit saw one `/careers` answer 200 on 2026-10-13 and 404 on 2026-10-14. A re-run on a later day will move a few companies. Re-verification ran about an hour after discovery.
- **`posted_at` is what each vendor reports**: Greenhouse `first_published`, Lever `createdAt`, Ashby `publishedAt`, SmartRecruiters `releasedDate`. A vendor that resets the date when a posting is edited or re-published understates age; evergreen postings overstate it. SmartRecruiters' 1,513-day median rests on 26 postings.
- **Documented-API coverage.** Workday and Oracle are recorded as `unsupported` and never polled (ADR 0003); the other unsupported vendors and YC Work at a Startup are recorded only (ADR 0030). Coverage means "has a board on one of the four documented public ATS APIs", not "is hiring". The following count as no board:
  - a careers page rendered only by client-side JavaScript whose scripts discovery does not read;
  - a board linked from a page discovery does not visit;
  - self-hosted listings.
- **Concentration.** The five largest boards hold 49.7% of open postings and the largest alone 27.5%, so posting-weighted shares lean toward a few companies. The board-weighted and largest-board-removed rows in section 3 show how much.
- **YC-active bias.** The cohort is companies YC lists as Active, which skews young, small and US-based. "Active" is YC's label and can lag a shutdown. The audit found board rates ranging from 37.9% for batches up to 2015 to 9.4% for 2024 and later. The results do not generalise to all companies or all startups, nor to SPEC section 4's 60-75% expectation for venture-backed US software companies.
- **Role families and interns** come from tell's title taxonomy (`tell/signals/taxonomy.py`); "other" is everything no family keyword matched.
- **Duplicates** use the requisition fingerprint, so two genuinely separate openings with identical title, department, location and remote flag also count.

## What this snapshot does not measure

Velocity (`new7` against a baseline), reposts and posting lifetimes all need two or more polls. This is the first poll on this database, so all 3,647 stored postings are open (2,625 of them on supported boards), and none is closed or reposted.

## Reproduce

```
export UV_PROJECT_ENVIRONMENT=$HOME/.venvs/tell UV_NO_SYNC=1
export DATABASE_URL=postgresql+psycopg://localhost:5432/<fresh database>
uv run alembic upgrade head
# sample: filter + random.Random(20261013).sample as described under Cohort
uv run tell discover seed-yc --file yc_sample.json
uv run tell discover batch --limit 1000 --rps 4   # repeat until no company lacks an outcome
for v in greenhouse lever ashby smartrecruiters; do uv run tell ingest --source $v; done
uv run tell discover reverify-guesses              # v3: ADR 0031 ownership evidence for guesses
```

With ADR 0031 in the code from the start, discovery applies the evidence rule itself. A guess without evidence is then paused before the poll, so the last line should pause nothing. This run applied the rule after the poll, which is why sections 2-6 exclude the paused boards' postings.
