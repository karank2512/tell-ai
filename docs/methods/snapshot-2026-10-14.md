# Snapshot 2026-10-14: 1,000 active YC companies

One reproducible measurement of what tell sees on a random sample of active Y Combinator companies: how many have a job board tell can read, where the others hire, what is on the boards, how old the open postings are, and which roles and tools they mention. It re-runs the 2026-10-13 snapshot on the same 1,000 companies with the fixed collectors and the revised discovery, on a fresh database. The work ran 2026-10-14 06:00Z to 07:17Z. Every percentage has its numerator and denominator beside it. Single snapshot: no velocity, reposts or posting lifetimes, because those need at least two polls.

## Question

For a random sample of active YC companies, what share has a job board on a documented public ATS API, where do the others point, and, for the boards tell polled once, how many open postings are there, how stale are they, what is the role mix, which tech keywords appear, and how often is the same requisition listed twice?

## Cohort

The same 1,000 companies as the 2026-10-13 snapshot, from the same sample file.

- **Population.** The public YC directory mirror `https://yc-oss.github.io/api/companies/all.json`, downloaded once at 2026-10-14T03:10:17Z, 6,297 records, SHA-256 `8c237f109a33b9f2e36616dbcdc8c4b72c2a1f3f437c23b671c97e327018f411`.
- **Filter.** `status == "Active"` and `website` a non-empty string after stripping whitespace: **4,337** companies.
- **Sample.** Population sorted ascending by YC `id` (then `slug`), then `random.Random(20261013).sample(population, 1000)` (CPython 3.13.2). **N = 1,000.** Only `id`, `slug`, `name`, `website`, `batch`, `all_locations` and `status` were kept.
- **Seeding.** `tell discover seed-yc --file yc_sample.json` (the 2026-10-13 file, unchanged): read 1,000, inserted 1,000, skipped for unusable website 0, duplicate domains 0.

## Procedure

All against a fresh local database created for this run (`createdb`, alembic head `0007`, all tables empty before seeding). Code: commit `cda2fdc` with uncommitted fixes (the collector contract fix and the discovery changes listed under "What changed since 2026-10-13").

| Measure | explicit SQL, run once after the poll (07:17:32Z) | - |

Discovery used one shared 4 requests/second budget for every request (robots.txt, company pages, scripts, vendor APIs), honoured robots.txt, and recorded zero errors. The ATS polls ran at each collector's default rate, recorded 0 errors, and skipped 0 jobs for failing validation.

**As-of.** Every posting age is measured against its own board's poll time (`ats_boards.last_polled_at`, the start of that vendor's ingest run, all within 07:16:12Z-07:17:19Z on 2026-10-14).

## What changed since 2026-10-13

The 2026-10-13 snapshot (same sample, alembic head and rate budget) is withdrawn from this directory because its sections 2-6 were partial. Three code changes separate the runs:

- **Collector contract fix.** Ashby `isRemote` and Greenhouse `location.name` may be `null`; on 2026-10-13 that failed 35 of 101 Ashby boards and 1 of 36 Greenhouse boards outright. A job that still fails validation is now skipped and counted instead of failing its board. Result here: 0 of 158 boards failed to parse and 0 jobs were skipped.
- **Discovery recall (ADR 0030).** When the first answering page names no ATS, discovery also reads the homepage, up to 2 same-site careers links and up to 5 same-site scripts; it records 18 more ATS vendors and YC Work at a Startup as `unsupported` instead of `no_board`; and it records Notion, Google Docs/Forms and LinkedIn links on the `no_board` marker and skips slug guesses after them. Ownership verification and the slug list are unchanged.
- **Concurrent discovery.** Six domains in flight sharing the one 4 rps budget, a 22-minute budget per batch, and a commit per domain.

## Caveats

- **True coverage is estimated, not measured.** A hand audit of the 2026-10-13 run (40 random no-board companies, 20 found boards) found 0 of 40 no-board companies with a supported board discovery missed (Wilson 95% upper bound 8.8%). Its implied true supported-board coverage on this population is **16.1%, 95% interval 16.1% to 23.3%** (161 + 820 x 0 to 8.8%, of 1,000); correcting for wrong boards (17 of 20 found boards belonged to the right company, 6 of 9 guessed ones), about **13.7%**, plausible range about 9% to 22%. This run's 15.8% is below that audit interval (fewer guessed boards are accepted now) and inside the wrong-board-corrected range. Guessed boards are still verified only by name: the 52 guessed boards here are the ones most likely to belong to another company.
- **One company has no recorded outcome.** Its discovery outcome is `unsupported` (Gem), but the Gem board identifier is already the board row of another sampled company, and discovery's unsupported write keeps the row's owner while overwriting its `discovered_from`. The company therefore never gets a row and was picked again by each later batch; the run summaries count it three times (1,002 outcomes for 1,000 companies). It is the "no outcome" row in section 1. A fix belongs in `tell/discovery/ats.py`.
- **One Ashby board answered 404** on the poll although discovery found it linked from the company's page; it is counted as supported and not polled.
- **Slug guesses now depend on the scan.** A company whose page points only at YC Work at a Startup or an unsupported ATS is no longer guessed; 7 companies with a guessed board on 2026-10-13 moved to `unsupported` this way. Some of those guesses may have been other companies' boards; this run cannot tell which.
- **Single snapshot.** One discovery pass and one poll. Nothing here is a trend. Pages change between days (the audit saw one `/careers` answer 200 on 2026-10-13 and 404 on 2026-10-14), so a re-run on a later day will move a few companies.
- **`posted_at` is what each vendor reports** (Greenhouse `first_published`, Lever `createdAt`, Ashby `publishedAt`, SmartRecruiters `releasedDate`). A vendor that resets the date when a posting is edited or re-published understates age; evergreen postings overstate it. SmartRecruiters' 1,521-day median rests on 30 postings.
- **Documented-API coverage.** Workday and Oracle are recorded as `unsupported`, never polled (ADR 0003); the other unsupported vendors and YC Work at a Startup are recorded only (ADR 0030). Coverage means "has a board on one of the four documented public ATS APIs", not "is hiring". A careers page rendered only by client-side JavaScript whose scripts discovery does not read, a board linked from a page discovery does not visit, or self-hosted listings count as no board.
- **Concentration.** The five largest boards hold 47.8% of open postings and the largest alone 19.8%, so posting-weighted shares lean toward a few companies. The board-weighted and largest-board-removed rows in section 3 show how much.
- **YC-active bias.** The cohort is companies YC lists as Active, which skews young, small and US-based; "Active" is YC's label and can lag a shutdown. The audit found board rates from 37.9% for batches up to 2015 to 9.4% for 2024 and later. Results do not generalise to all companies or all startups, nor to SPEC section 4's 60-75% expectation for venture-backed US software companies.
- **Role families and interns** come from tell's title taxonomy (`tell/signals/taxonomy.py`); "other" is everything no family keyword matched.
- **Duplicates** use the requisition fingerprint; two genuinely separate openings with identical title, department, location and remote flag also count.

## What this snapshot does not measure

Velocity (`new7` against a baseline), reposts and posting lifetimes all need two or more polls; this is the first poll on this database, so all 3,647 postings are open and none is closed or reposted.
